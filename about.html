    The Ambition

    As a Professional Developer, I practice accessibility, maintainability, and optimization in my work. I use Agile methodology with heavy emphasis on version control and system blueprinting to alleviate some of the weaknesses of Agile. My ambition for this website was a place for me to share my work and promote myself. I believe when incorporating someone into your project, it not only matters their work but who they are and what they stand for. On this website you’ll find most of the projects I’ve worked on in the past. I try to add detail to every single entry to share my process and hopefully inspire others.

    This website is a static website hosted on an AWS S3 bucket. When creating this web page I wanted something I could easily update, as one of the risks I wanted to avoid was a messy pipeline. I use VSCode and Github for versioning ensuring my website is always up to date. Deployment is handled via a Github action, which holds permissions to upload to my static S3 bucket upon committing. Lastly, updates are verified and deployed through AWS Amplify.

    I try to use in-house as much as possible when developing my own work. I believe understanding the whole system allows for better development and helps me grow as a developer. Every html block, CSS line, and artwork piece is made by me, reviewed by me, and incorporated by me. There are moments when I find it hard to adapt, but reinforcing my skillset and mind allow me to overcome obstacles and deliver a quality of work I am proud of.

    Jason Lopez
